(title) raf simons: fourth sex

(description) Published in 2003, this thematic volume delves into adolescence, gender fluidity, and countercultural identity through Simons’s lens. Featuring a compelling visual narrative from the The Fourth Sex collection—comprising androgynous silhouettes, youth rebellion motifs, and editorial photo essays—it remains a touchstone for discerning Raf Simons collectors.

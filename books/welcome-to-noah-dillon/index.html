(title) Welcome to Noah Dillon

(description) A rare, first-edition 36-page softcover photo book by independent lensman Noah Dillon, published by Welcome.jpeg. Through raw, cinematic photographs, Dillon captures intimate portraits, urban landscapes, and the poetic undercurrents of city life. The book presents a quietly powerful visual narrative—laying bare the textures of his surroundings with a restrained but evocative aesthetic. Prized for its authenticity and limited print run, it offers a compelling glimpse into Dillon’s photographic vision.

(title) The truth of number (n)ine

(description) The Tokyo-based menswear brand NUMBER (N)INE announced its disbandment following designer Takahiro Miyashita’s departure, revealed in a letter dated February 20. The decision came after the brand’s final presentation, “A Closed Feeling,” at the Paris Collections, shocking the fashion industry at the height of its popularity and creativity. Amid rumors and criticism, EYESCREAM magazine seeks to uncover the true reasons behind the brand’s end through Miyashita’s direct cooperation in a special feature titled “The Truth of Number (N)ine.”

(title) ROCKS: Rei kawakubo, Yonko Ono Special Issue

(description) “ROCKS: Rei kawakubo, Yonko ono Special Issue” from 2010. It focuses on rock culture and the idea of individuality, emphasizing a lifestyle that does not follow trends or fashion. The cover features two Japanese rock artists shown in bold, yellow toned portraits, creating a strong and rebellious visual style. A handwritten message on the cover explains that rock represents an unchanging way of life, reinforcing the theme of authenticity and staying true to one’s spirit.

(title) Vivienne Westwood: Fashion Memoir (1997, Gene Krell)

(description) This compact yet potent 79‑page illustrated memoir captures Westwood’s essence as the “Queen of Punk.” With photographs and texts by Gene Krell, it reflects her punk origins, theatrical irreverence, and later impact on global fashion. Noted for its celebration of her boundary-pushing creativity and iconoclastic spirit.

(title) Rick Owens SWITCH Magazine

(description) This bilingual (Japanese/English), 134-page special issue of SWITCH Magazine is dedicated entirely to Rick Owens’s creative universe. Part editorial survey, part documentary, it explores his multi-disciplinary inspirations—from furniture and performance to butoh and interior design. Packed with photo stories, design essays, and interviews, it captures the raw intensity and intellectual depth of Owens’s work.

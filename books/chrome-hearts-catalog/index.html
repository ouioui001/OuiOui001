(title) Chrome Hearts Brutus extra

(description) Chrome Hearts Brutus magazine,featuring editorial photos of the jewelry/fashion brand chrome hearts, founded in 1988 in Los Angeles by Richard Stark, Leonard Kamhout, and John Bowman. Chrome Hearts is known for its distinctive rock-and-roll, gothic, and biker influences. Initially starting in a small garage creating custom motorcycle gear for friends in LA. Currently led by Richard Stark and Laurie Lynn Stark, the brand extends into furniture, jewelry, leather pieces, and homeware, with a presence worldwide.

(title) Hedi Slimane Pen Magazine

(description) Hedi Slimane a figure who is focusing on his dramatic return to Saint Laurent and the shockwaves caused by his debut menswear collection in January 2013. Moving from photography back into fashion, Slimane rejected explanation or compromise, presenting a raw, rock-infused vision that sharply divided opinion but never faded from discussion. His work blurred the line between fashion and personal life, treating creation as something instinctive rather than intellectual, and aimed not to preserve tradition but to dismantle and rebuild it. Ultimately, the piece portrays Slimane as a solitary, uncompromising designer who relentlessly pulls fashion toward a colder, sharper, and more radical future.

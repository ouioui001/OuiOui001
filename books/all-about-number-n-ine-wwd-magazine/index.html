(title) All about Number (N)ine WWD Magazine

(description) All about Number (N)ine WWD Magazine 2006–07 Autumn/Winter Collection captures designer Takahiro Miyashita’s return to his roots. Presented in Paris, the show featured 36 meticulously tailored black looks, marching an elegant departure from the brand’s typical “street casual” image. Miyashita emphasized his pride in traditional tailoring, making this season one of his most personal and refined expressions. The accompanying publication documents every look from the show along with an exclusive interview offering insight into his creative vision at this pivotal moment in Number (N)ine’s history.

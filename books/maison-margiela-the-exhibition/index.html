(title) Maison Martin Margiela: The Exhibition

(description) Produced to accompany the 2008 retrospective exhibit, this catalog immerses the reader in Martin Margiela’s world of atelier process, Tabi shoe molds, and the defining “circled-number” system. Sections explore themes like Incognito, Body, Paint, Tailoring—and spotlight Margiela’s obsession with anonymity and white-painted garments. Featuring cryptic imagery and insider commentary, it feels like a studio tour in book form.

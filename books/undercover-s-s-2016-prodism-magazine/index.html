(title) UNDERCOVER S/S 2016 PRODISM Magazine

(description) UNDERCOVER’s Spring/Summer 2016 collection, “THE GREATEST,” created to celebrate the brand’s 25th anniversary. It highlights Jun Takahashi’s approach to fashion & the brand’s continued global influence since its founding. The feature positions this season as a convergence of past and present, while the PRODISM issue gave an in-depth exploration of UNDERCOVER through previews, styling, product reviews, and extensive questioning that examines the brand’s true essence from multiple angles.

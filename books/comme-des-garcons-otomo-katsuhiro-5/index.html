(title) Comme des garcons Otomo Katsuhiro #5

(description) Each year, Comme des Garçons collaborates with an artist to mark the new fashion season. For Spring/Summer 2013, the brand released a 25-issue catalogue that compiles into a comic, designed by founder Rei Kawakubo. The publication features collage works by Katsuhiro Otomo & Joseph Crocker, with Otomo Katsuhiro becoming the first Japanese artist to appear in the series.

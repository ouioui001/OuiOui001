(title) Comme des garcons memoire de la mode

(description) Since her groundbreaking 1981 Paris debut, Rei Kawakubo has redefined fashion through her avant-garde Comme des Garçons designs blurring the line between clothing and sculpture. In this book, “Comme des garcons memoire de la mode” Rei Kawakubo's fashion background and history was highlighted. Rei Kawakubo's rise as a self-taught visionary who reshaped both global fashion and modern Japan was well documented in this book.

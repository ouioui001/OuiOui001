(title) raf simons redux

(description) A definitive 2005 retrospective marking Raf Simons’s first decade in fashion. This high-quality hardcover (≈ 25.5 × 32.5 cm) features over 224 pages of editorial visuals by Willy Vanderperre, Collier Schorr, Ronald Stoops, and more. It spans Simons’s archival collections such as Consumed and Riot! Riot! Riot!, offering a photo-rich narrative of youth and counterculture-inspired tailoring. With contextual essays on his philosophy and evolution, this large-format tome is prized as both a research resource and a stunning art object.

(title) Thought Without concept: Carol Christian Poell's Paradoxical Aesthetics

(description) Carol Christian Poell is an Austrian designer based in Milan. Born in Linz in 1966, he trained as a tailor in Vienna and studied fashion design at Domus Academy, where he co-founded C.C.P. srl. Since debuting his menswear collection in 1995, he has presented his work selectively through performances, distancing himself from the traditional fashion system.

(title) Numéro Tokyo Homme Yuta In Balenciaga

(description) Yuta was featured on the front and back covers of the April 2022 Special Edition of Numéro TOKYO, released on February 26, 2022, along with an exclusive 16-page insert, Numéro TOKYO Homme. In his interview, he reflected on living authentically, overcoming challenges, and forging his own path. He also shared a personal story about his father telling him “don’t ever come back” when he left for Korea, a remark that motivated him to work harder. The accompanying photoshoot highlighted his striking visual appeal through bold, moody, and sophisticated fashion imagery.

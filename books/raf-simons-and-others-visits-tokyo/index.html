(title) RAF SIMONS & others visits Tokyo

(description) Raf Simons during a trip to Tokyo, where he reflects on creativity, daily life, and unexpected encounters. Through meetings with artists, friends, exhibitions, food, and city walks, he observes Japan’s colors, moods, and cultural details with curiosity and warmth. The trip becomes less about fashion and more about connection, inspiration, and the quiet feeling of being at ease in a place that feels both unfamiliar and strangely like home.

(title) A Magazine Curated by Yohji Yamamoto

(description) A Magazine Curated by Yohji Yamamoto is widely regarded as one of the most beautiful single issues ever produced. Conceived as an artful extension of Yamamoto’s world, it blends fashion, photography, and philosophy across roughly 180 pages. The issue features contributions from Nick Knight, Rinko Kawauchi, Peter Lindbergh, Paolo Roversi, and more, alongside intimate conversations with Hans-Ulrich Obrist and Wim Wenders. Designed with the refinement of an art book, it captures Yamamoto’s poetic vision through striking imagery and thoughtful editorial direction.

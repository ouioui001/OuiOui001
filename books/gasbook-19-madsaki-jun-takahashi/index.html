(title) GASBOOK 19 MADSAKI JUN TAKAHASHI

(description) Jun Takahashi, founder of the acclaimed fashion label UNDERCOVER, & MADSAKI, the Osaka-born, New York–raised artist affiliated with Takashi Murakami’s Kaikai Kiki, come together in this rare collaboration showcasing their works. Jun Takahashi, from launching UNDERCOVER while at Bunka Fashion College to earning multiple Mainichi Fashion Grand Prix awards and presenting in Tokyo, Paris, and Pitti Uomo, illustrates his evolution into one of Japan’s most influential designers. MADSAKI, an artist whose style merges satire, vulnerability, and graffiti-driven critique, brings a perspective shaped by bicultural identity and the international art scene. Together, the book captures the creative synergy of two boundary pushing figures in contemporary fashion and art.

(title) Black TAKAHIROMIYASHITA The Soloist. SS2017

(description) TakahiroMiyashitaTheSoloist. Spring/Summer 2017 black cover channels the spirit of David Bowie, reflecting designer Takahiro Miyashita’s deep musical influences and fascination with artistic reinvention. Inspired by Bowie’s ever-evolving personas, the collection explored transformation and identity through Miyashita’s signature layered tailoring and expressive styling. Marking a new chapter for the brand. now semi-officially renamed TakahiroMiyashitaTheSoloist., this season stood out as one of the designer’s most confident and conceptually unified works since his Number (N)ine era.

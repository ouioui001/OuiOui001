(title) helmut lang 032c magazine

(description) Helmut Lang revolutionized fashion by dismantling traditional rules and reimagining how clothing is made, shown, and perceived. Known for countless industry firsts, from artist collaborations to coed runway shows and pioneering backstage photography, Lang combined rigor with radical vision. 032c Issue 31 dedicates a 48-page dossier to The Helmut Lang Legacy, exploring the lasting impact of his abrupt 2005 exit through essays, a roundtable with leading fashion voices, an interview with Lang, and rare archival material that highlights his enduring influence.

(title) Comme des garcons Rizzoli

(description) Rei Kawakubo and Comme des Garçons by Deyan Sudjic, published by Rizzoli 1990, offers a concise yet immersive portrait of Kawakubo as a designer whose influence reaches far beyond clothing. Drawing on behind-the-scenes glimpses of studios, textile mills, and retail spaces, the book captures her holistic creative world, encompassing garments, furniture, and the radical interiors of Comme des Garçons stores.

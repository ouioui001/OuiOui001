(title) Undercover: Jun Takahashi

(description) A lavish 256‑page Rizzoli monograph by Suzy Menkes, this is the first comprehensive book on Jun Takahashi’s work, charting his journey from Ura-Harajuku beginnings to Parisian acclaim. Packed with over 200 photographs, fashion essays, archival sketches, and insights from collaborators, it highlights the designer’s transformation from punk‑inspired collage to refined, sculptural elegance.

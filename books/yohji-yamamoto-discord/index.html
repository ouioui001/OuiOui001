(title) Yohji Yamamoto Discord

(description) discord Yohji Yamamoto expresses Yamamoto's design philosophy in the form of luxury accessories. Through this collection of high-quality bags, he continues to make design innovations in the world of fashion, armed with a spirit of rebellion against the status quo. The name of the brand was chosen to reflect his philosophy of unfinished and asymmetric aesthetics. The brand aims to transmit Yamamoto's dignified and unparalleled perspective from Japan to the world.

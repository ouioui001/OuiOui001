(title) White TAKAHIROMIYASHITATheSoloist. SS2017

(description) The counterpart to the Black edition, this Spring/Summer 2017 volume emphasizes pure white aesthetics. It explores the same themes—Zen minimalism, modular draping, Bowie‑inspired youth rebellion—through light-filled editorial images. It's a study in contrast and form, capturing The Soloist’s disciplined elegance in stark monochrome.
